One Voice — “The kitchen table”

Art-direction review for level 0 · original screen-print / inked-poster look · procedural pipeline mockup (no third-party art, no CDNs)

01Art direction

The feeling

A late kitchen where frightened neighbours slowly find each other. It should read like a hand-pulled political print — warm, human, a little rough — not a UI or a cartoon.

One authored look that scales to every later district by swapping props, palette accents and the environment, never the system.

How the look is built

  • Ink line with imperfect, displaced edges (screen-print misregistration).
  • Halftone dots for shading instead of gradients.
  • Paper grain multiplied over the whole frame.
  • Warm pools of light from lamp, oil flame and windows; deep readable shadow elsewhere.
  • Limited palette — a handful of inks and cloths, tinted per person.

Do

  • Strong silhouettes, visible faces and gestures.
  • Purposeful composition: the table centres the eye, depth front-to-back.
  • State you can feel: silent → awakening → acting reads at a glance.
  • Restrained texture — grain supports, never shouts.

Avoid

  • Generic vector icons, glossy cards, drop-shadow UI.
  • Photorealism, neon, military aesthetics.
  • Excessive detail that fights readability at speed.
  • Off-the-shelf game art or any remote asset dependency.

03Story states

Silent — closed windows, isolated people, heavy shadow. No one has spoken yet.
Awakening — courtyard windows warm, conversations begin, people turn toward one another and lean in.
Acting together — the room is lit, the courtyard wakes, clarity spreads; a persistent trace lantern remains.

04Character construction

Modular parts — one silhouette, tinted garment, tinted head, a hair style, then prop & face — assemble into distinct, expressive neighbours; posture and expression shift with awareness (afraid → listening → clear).

05Environment layers

Layered composition — the scene stacks back-to-front (walls & floor → windows & courtyard → shelf, lamp & table → cast, near rim, light & paper), so any later district reskins the same slots.
